We introduce CanonDressGS, a canonical-space framework for clothing-conditioned Gaussian human avatars with LHM-assisted priors. The method builds a canonical naked Gaussian base avatar from THuman/SMPL-X white-body priors, learns clothing-conditioned canonical Gaussian offsets with a lightweight HyperNetwork, and reuses MMLPHuman-style pose-driven deformation and Gaussian rendering for novel pose and novel view synthesis. LHM is used offline as a teacher prior to provide dressed Gaussian geometry supervision through SMPL-X surface-aware anchor alignment.
