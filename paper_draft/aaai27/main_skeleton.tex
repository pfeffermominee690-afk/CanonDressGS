\begin{abstract}
We study reference-controlled wardrobe editing for one fixed animatable Gaussian avatar and five preregistered seen garments. The method separates a frozen explicit canonical residual basis from a reference-only coefficient predictor, while pose and camera enter only the downstream deformation and rendering stages. This design targets a failure mode in which shared neural decoding smooths or collapses localized high-frequency garment residuals. The basis has $K=4$, the complete centered rank available from five garment endpoints, and is not presented as strong low-rank compression.
