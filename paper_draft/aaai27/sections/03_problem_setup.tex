\section{Problem Setup}

\paragraph{Fixed avatar and canonical support.}
Let $\mathcal{A}_0$ denote one fixed-identity animatable avatar with $N=200{,}000$ canonical Gaussians. Its canonical state is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{G}_0=(X_0,S_0,Q_0,O_0,H_0,H_{>0}),
\end{equation}
where position, log scaling, rotation, opacity logit, degree-zero spherical harmonics, and remaining spherical-harmonic coefficients share a fixed Gaussian index. The base state, Gaussian count, deformation model, and renderer are frozen in the paper protocol.

Clothing is represented by a six-channel canonical residual field
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{R}=(\Delta X,\Delta S,\Delta\Omega,\Delta O,\Delta H_0,\Delta H_{>0}),
\end{equation}
with shapes $[N,3]$, $[N,3]$, $[N,3]$, $[N]$, $[N,1,3]$, and $[N,3,3]$ in the frozen artifact. Rotation residuals use an axis-angle vector and are composed as a local quaternion update. A physical zero residual leaves the base avatar unchanged; it is distinct from the nonzero mean residual of the garment basis.

\paragraph{Closed seen wardrobe and teachers.}
The preregistered seen wardrobe is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{seen}}=\{\mathrm{O01},\mathrm{O02},\mathrm{O03},\mathrm{O04},\mathrm{O08}\},
\end{equation}
and each garment is observed in four fixed conditions corresponding to front, back, left, and right views. For garment $g$, multi-view residual optimization produces a teacher field $\mathcal{R}_g^{\star}$ on the same canonical Gaussian support. O07 is excluded from the seen wardrobe, coefficient normalization, basis construction, predictor fitting, threshold selection, and model selection. It is retained as a held-out failure probe and is never included in the seen-garment aggregate.

\paragraph{Reference-conditioned episode.}
For a target condition $t$, an episode supplies the other three observations of the same registered garment as a reference set
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{S}_t=\{(I_j,M_j^{\mathrm{cloth}},M_j^{\mathrm{fg}},v_j)\}_{j\neq t},
\qquad |\mathcal{S}_t|=3,
\end{equation}
where $v_j$ is a validity flag. The target provides pose and camera $(p_t,c_t)$ for downstream animation and rendering. Target RGB $I_t$ and target masks $M_t$ are used only to optimize garment-specific teachers, to supervise/evaluate rendered outputs, and to construct frozen evaluation records. They are forbidden inputs to the reference coefficient predictor.

The predictor produces a standardized coefficient vector using only the reference set,
\begin{equation}
\hat{\mathbf z}=f_{\theta}(\mathcal{S}_t), \qquad \hat{\mathbf z}\in\mathbb{R}^{4}.
\end{equation}
After de-standardization, the raw coefficient selects a canonical residual from a frozen explicit basis. Only then may $(p_t,c_t)$ be consumed by the frozen deformation and rendering path.

\paragraph{Direct and indirect target-view use.}
Reference--target image overlap is exactly zero within every prediction episode: the target condition is absent from $\mathcal{S}_t$, and the coefficient forward pass reads no target RGB, target mask, target pose, or target camera. This direct-input exclusion is not an end-to-end view-isolation result. Each teacher is optimized from all four target views, the explicit basis is constructed from those full-view teachers, and predictor fitting cycles over episodes spanning the same four conditions. Thus target-view information enters indirectly through teacher endpoints, the basis, and training supervision. We classify the evaluation as \textsc{View-Transductive}, not strict leave-one-view-out.

\paragraph{Evaluation unit.}
The frozen contract contains one correct episode for every seen-garment/target-condition pair. Metrics are computed per episode, averaged equally across the four conditions of each outfit, and then macro-averaged across the five outfits. Replicate or seed aggregation is applied only after this hierarchy. A deterministic zero-initialized method can have multiple protocol replicates without independently randomized initial states. No best-replicate selection is permitted.

\paragraph{Claim domain.}
The estimand is reference-controlled editing within the fixed five-garment wardrobe for this one avatar. O07, other garments, other identities, and strictly held-out-view systems are outside the positive claim domain.
