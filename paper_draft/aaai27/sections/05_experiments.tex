\section{Experiments}

\subsection{Protocol and questions}

All main experiments use one fixed avatar, five preregistered seen garments, four preregistered views, and target-disjoint three-reference episodes. The protocol remains view-transductive. O07 is a held-out failure probe and is never used for training, threshold selection, hyperparameter selection, or pilot tuning. Candidate values are not paper results until the unified evaluator, replicate aggregation, manual visual review, and final artifact freeze are complete.

We organize the evaluation around four questions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Does Ours-v2 improve on the base, mean-only, true outfit-ID lookup, reference-classifier lookup, nearest-centroid lookup, and a matched complex corrected branch?
  \item Which representation, reference, standardization, initialization, and supervision choices matter under the revised Ours-v2 contract?
  \item What are the teacher/onboarding, predictor, inference, rendering, storage, and peak-memory costs?
  \item Where does the closed-wardrobe representation fail, including O07 and spatial artifacts?
\end{enumerate}
