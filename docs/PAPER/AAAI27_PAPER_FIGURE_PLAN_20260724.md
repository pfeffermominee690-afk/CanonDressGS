## Selection rules

- No best-seed, best-garment, best-view, or post-result query selection.
- Rank panels show all ranks 1-4; reference panels show all counts 1-3.
- Main/baseline panels use equal containment, padding, background, and tile size.
- No asymmetric crop, color enhancement, sharpening, retouch, inpainting, or
  super-resolution.
- Failure panels require a registered trigger and denominator.
- Historical and pending protocols are never mixed without source labels.

Caption skeletons retain scientific question, panel order, metric definition,
provenance status, current boundary, replacement fields, and prohibited claims.
