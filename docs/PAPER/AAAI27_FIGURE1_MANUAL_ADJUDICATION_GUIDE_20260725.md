## Editorial Recommendation

## Decision Procedure

1. Inspect all four candidate PDFs, not only the contact sheet.
2. Check legibility at the intended two-column paper width.
3. Confirm that the candidate represents the closed-wardrobe scope without implying unseen-garment generalization.
4. Confirm that raw and snapped predictions are not conflated.
5. Record exactly one selection in `paper_draft/figure_review/figure1_manual_adjudication/figure1_manual_selection_form.md`.
6. Start the separate task `USER_SELECT_FIGURE1_CANDIDATE_THEN_INTEGRATE_AND_FINALIZE_SCOPE`.

Do not edit the candidate PDFs during adjudication. Any post-selection composition change must be regenerated by the deterministic asset builder and reflected in both transform and asset registries.

## Provenance Boundary

Composition uses proportional resizing only; it performs no crop, retouch, sharpening, synthesis, inference, training, or rendering. The registries record source and output SHA-256 values. Scientific source pixel mutation count is zero.
