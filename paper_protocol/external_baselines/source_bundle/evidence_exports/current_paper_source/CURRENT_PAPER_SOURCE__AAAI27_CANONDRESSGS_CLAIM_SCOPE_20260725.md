# CanonDressGS Claim Scope (2026-07-25)

## Supported Main Claim

For one fixed subject02 avatar and a closed bank of five seen garments, CanonDressGS predicts and realizes a valid registered canonical garment endpoint from seen-garment references while preserving the frozen deformation and renderer. The deployed output is a snapped endpoint, not the raw coefficient.

## Supported Analysis

- Geometry interpolation is the dominant cause of invalid intermediate garment states under the sealed ten-pair decomposition.
- Clean endpoint selection is exact in the registered condition-fold protocol and is functionally equivalent to registered hard lookup.
- Dual-Support improves all-pair aggregate metrics while keeping two valid supports separate, but its pair and weight are specified externally.
- Test-time coefficient refinement yields no gain.
- A four-garment rank-3 affine basis is capacity-limited for the held-out fifth garment.

## Unsupported Claims

The draft does not claim unseen-garment generation, open-world editing, accurate continuous control, a generalizable garment manifold, a second-identity result, cross-identity transfer, or successful automatic mixed-reference routing.
